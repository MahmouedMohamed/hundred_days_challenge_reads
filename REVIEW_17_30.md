# Review: days 17-30 (not yet published)

Folders 17/-30/ are rendered from `tools/src/17.json`-`30.json`. Please check each day before pushing; once pushed, a day can't be renumbered.
All of them are short quotes or verses, one page per language. None of them are Quran verses or hadith. Arabic originals are kept as written; the other four languages are translations written for this repo.
Confidence covers both the wording and the attribution.

## Themes

Resolve (17), courage (18), small steps (19), focusing on what you control (20), responsibility (21), kindness (22), gratitude (23), patience (24), habit and practice (25), family and mothers (26), using time well (27), reading (28), mercy (29), character (30).

## Rights

All of the authors died long ago. The translations used as the base for the English (Higginson, Maude, Ross) are public domain, and every other translation was written for this repo.

## Day 16 (already committed)

Day 16 (ميت الأحياء) has no `source`. The verse "ليس من مات فاستراح بميت / إنما الميت ميت الأحياء" is usually attributed to Adi ibn al-Ra'la al-Ghassani. I could not confirm where the first line, "قد مات قوم وهم في الناس أحياء", comes from; it does not seem to belong to the same verse. Consider adding a credit for the verse.
